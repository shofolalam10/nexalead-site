Ask us directly
Will it sound like a robot?

You hear it and approve it before launch. We choose a natural voice and write the scripts around how you talk to customers.

What happens if it cannot help a caller?

It takes a detailed message or transfers the call to you, based on rules you set. You get a summary either way.

Can it book straight into my calendar?

Yes. It books into the calendar you already use, or texts the caller a link to pick a time.

Do I have to change my phone number?

No. Your number stays the same. Calls forward to your receptionist all the time or only when you cannot pick up.

How long does setup take?

Most businesses are live within one to two weeks, depending on how many tools we connect.

What does the setup fee cover?

Building your receptionist, writing and testing scripts, and connecting your calendar and tools before launch.

Am I locked into a contract?

No. Plans are month-to-month and you can cancel with 30 days notice.

What if I go over my call limit?

Nothing stops working. Extra calls are $0.75 each, and we will recommend a better plan if you are regularly over.

Do you guarantee results?

We do not promise a set number of jobs, since that depends on your market. We do make sure every call is answered and every lead is followed up.
